CASE 01 Social Discourse · Cognitive Steelmanning · Video Scripting

Kritik Internalisasi Sexism

Al Fihri Institute

01 · Systemic Problem

Many women still belittle other women, in education, romance and career, as an internalized form of patriarchal culture. Moralizing campaigns trigger instant psychological defensiveness and fail.

02 · Cross-Domain Insight

Cognitive psychology and social observation: people drop their guard when they feel recognized, not judged. The phenomenon has to be shown before it is criticized.

03 · Copy Execution

A narrator hook that stops the scroll, then a cut to a scene embodying internalized sexism through the "pick me" archetype. A two-character dialogue then peels back its psychological roots, closing with a reflective-question CTA.

04 · Impact

A taboo topic reframed as participatory public discourse, inviting reflection in the comments instead of resistance. [Add engagement metrics: views, comments, shares]

CASE 02 Community Advocacy · Information Architecture · Grassroots Mobilization

Desa Bercerita

Mekarjaya · Founder & Lead

01 · Systemic Problem

The friction was not between village bureaucracy and farmers. It was internal: between the community's own team and the surrounding residents and farmers.

02 · Cross-Domain Insight

Communication is a routing problem. Separate the channels by function and each audience receives the language it can actually use.

03 · Execution

All bureaucratic channels centralized in the founder. The PR division and mentors act as cultural translators, simplifying the program's concepts for local residents and farmers.

04 · Impact

Around 20 volunteers and hundreds of residents moving in sync across the program. [Confirm exact figures & duration]
